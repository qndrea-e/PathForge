🎁 Reward Cards

Fortune sometimes favors prepared engineers. Reward Cards provide powerful advantages.

🛡️

Safe Card

Become immune to one Punishment Card.

🚧

Block Card

Receive one Block Tile to obstruct another player's path.

⛏️

Remove Card

Remove one Forge Tile from each opposing player.

🔄

Wish Card

Swap your path with another player.

🎲

Choose Card

Choose to draw another card or gain one Punishment Immunity.

➕

Gain Card

Receive two additional Forge Tiles.

⚠️ Punishment Cards

Even the greatest engineers face unexpected setbacks.

🎁

Dawors Card

Every other player takes one Forge Tile from you.

⏸️

Missed Card

Gain no tile and skip your next turn.

🤝

Give Card

Give one of your Forge Tiles to another player.

🎮 How Cards Work

① Draw

Draw the top card from the deck.

② Reveal

Determine whether it is a Challenge, Reward, or Punishment Card.

③ Resolve

Answer the puzzle or apply the card effect.

④ Build

Use earned Forge Tiles to build your path or block an opponent.
