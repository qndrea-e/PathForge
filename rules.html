Rulebook

Master the rules. Forge your strategy. Escape Mechatria.

🎯 Objective

Build a continuous path from your starting point to the Gate of Mastery using Forge Tiles earned through puzzles and special cards. The first player to reach the Gate wins.

👥 Players

2–4

Players

Compete to build your own path while preventing your opponents from reaching the gate.

1

Game Master

Reads challenge cards, verifies answers using the Book of Everything, and resolves Reward and Punishment cards.

🎮 Gameplay

① Draw

Draw one card from the deck.

② Solve

If it is a Challenge Card, answer the puzzle.

③ Verify

The Game Master checks your answer using the Book of Everything.

④ Earn

Correct answers earn Forge Tiles. Reward and Punishment cards activate immediately.

⑤ Build

Place your Forge Tile and end your turn.

🧱 Forge Tile Rules

⬅➡

Horizontal

Tiles may be placed horizontally.

⬆⬇

Vertical

Tiles may also be placed vertically.

❌

No Diagonal

Diagonal placement is strictly prohibited.

🧩 Challenge Cards

✅

Correct Answer

Gain one Forge Tile. Use it to extend your path or strategically block another player.

❌

Incorrect Answer

Gain no Forge Tile. Your turn immediately ends.

🏆 Winning the Game

A player wins immediately upon creating a continuous path to the Gate of Mastery.


If all cards have already been drawn, the winner is the player whose path is closest to the Gate of Mastery.


If a player's path can no longer possibly reach the Gate, that player is immediately disqualified.

📜 Remember

Knowledge earns the tile.

Ethics builds the path.

Integrity opens the gate.
