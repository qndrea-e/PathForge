What is PlayForge?

[ Insert Board Game Image Here ]

PlayForge is an educational strategy board game inspired by Mechatronics Engineering in Academe. Players compete by answering engineering-themed challenges, collecting Forge Tiles, overcoming unexpected events, and strategically constructing the path toward freedom.

Game Features

🧩

5 Puzzle Categories

Wordplay, Riddles, Jumbled Letters, Find the Impostor, and Conversion Challenges.

🛡️

Reward Cards

Gain powerful advantages that can change the outcome of the game.

⚠️

Punishment Cards

Unexpected setbacks that challenge your strategy.

🧱

Forge Tiles

Build your own path or strategically block opponents.

🎯

Strategic Gameplay

Every decision affects your journey toward the Gate of Mastery.

👥

Multiplayer

Designed for 2–4 players with one Game Master.
